# Storylines

**Picked: A.** It is the only one built around the feature the person named first (seeking a video in a window that isn't focused), shown as a moment a stranger recognises.

## A. Hands stay on your work (picked)

Device: a moment in someone's day, then the feature as the carrier. Form: object-to-object chain (the video becomes the stage, the player becomes the icon).
Someone takes notes in a document while a film plays in a browser window behind it. They scroll over the film without clicking; it rewinds and the document never loses focus. The camera dives into the film, which becomes a bright studio stage where every other control performs on the same player, one per two beats, until the player floats away to the corner and turns into the app icon.

- First 2 s: a light desktop, a Notes window in front with a caret typing, a browser window behind playing *Spring*; "Video in the background." blurs in word by word.
- Beats: hook (desktop) → proof (scroll rewinds, focus stays) → dive into the film → 3× / ¼× → loop, mark, boost, speed, dim, tint, snap, float → icon + Chrome and Firefox.
- Look: Apple-light (#F5F5F7 stage, #1D1D1F ink), Inter Display tight, the brand's blue-violet only on the icon and one word.
- Sound: bright 120 BPM electronic bed; whoosh on the dive, ticks on key caps, shutter on the screenshot, riser into the icon.

## B. Type carries it

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant verbs ("Scroll." "Rewind." "Loop." "Float.") fill a white frame and cut on every half beat, each one punched through by a macro crop of the real control doing that verb.

## C. One line

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: match cuts.
The extension's thin seek bar is the only constant: it scrubs a film, bends into a loop range, stands up as the volume pill, stretches into the speed track, wraps into the mini-player's frame and finally folds into the icon.
